Notes & best practices:

Respect robots.txt and rate limits. Throttle requests.

Use caching headers and conditional GET (If-Modified-Since) to reduce bandwidth.

For production: separate fetch worker (Celery + Redis) and queue system. Here APScheduler is acceptable for MVP.

Monitor errors and add retry/backoff (aiohttp + exponential backoff).

Rotate user-agent and consider proxy if blocked.

For images: prefer downloading and serving from storage (S3 / Supabase) to avoid hotlink failures.

Implement admin UI to manage sources, view errors, and force re-fetches.
